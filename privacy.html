Privacy policy

Last updated September 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Chirag Duhoon, hosted on GitHub Pages. It exists to show my projects, achievements and certifications. This page explains what data is and is not collected when you visit.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. The site has no accounts, no forms, no comments, no analytics scripts and no advertising. It does not set cookies of its own.

Hosting and third-party requests

Loading the site makes requests to a few third parties, each of which may log standard technical information such as your IP address, browser and the time of the request under their own policies:

  • GitHub Pages, which serves the site.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the Geist typeface.
  • unpkg, a CDN that serves the Phosphor icon stylesheet.

External links

The site links to GitHub, LinkedIn, LeetCode, Instagram and a live project demo hosted on Render. Those sites have their own privacy policies and I do not control what they collect.

Contact

If you email me at chiragduhoon.work@gmail.com, I will only use your message and address to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will change with it.
